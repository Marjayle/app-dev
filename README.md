## My Favorite Series: Kakegurui

My favorite series is **Kakegurui**. It's an anime about a rich private school where students rank each other by gambling instead of grades. 

### Why I like it:
- The mind games and plot twists are crazy
- **Yumeko Jabami** is unhinged and literally just gambles for the thrill of it
- The intense facial expressions and animation style are super iconic and funny

> "Let's lose ourselves to gambling."
